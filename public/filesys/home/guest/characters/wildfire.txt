Supposedly born to two poverty stricken miners before the current boom of activity in Soundgarden,
E'laya was abandoned on the Last Gasp by his parents at a young age. Because of this, his exact parentage is unknown.
Instead, E'laya became a child of the station, relying on the generosity of the close-knit community which existed
there prior to the sudden new wave of researchers, clients and companies in the system.
Having somewhat above average intelligence for his age and a curiosity for the machines that powered the station,
he quickly became at home among the technicians and engineers of the station, and it was not uncommon to see him
hanging around in their offices and workstations, or tagging along with them while they performed maintenance
or repairs on the station or its many mining rigs.
