Station that is mostly populated by miners and scientists, with a small percentage of the economy dedicated to tourism (Pre-Recall Rush).
Since the findings of Recall and other scientific marvels the population of LGS has ballooned from
a comfortable ~50,000 souls to upwards of ~90,000 harboured on the station,
with a further ~25,000 anchored to LGS via cables and living in various sized ships.
